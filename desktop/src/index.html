Study title: Evaluating Minecraft as educational tool

In this study, you will play our custom educational Minecraft game, answer questions about your experience, take a knowledge test about the fantasy creatures in the game, and provide an email address for a short follow-up questionnaire about one week later. The session takes about 50 minutes in total (35 min in-game + 15 min questions).

You can only take part if you are 16 years or older and currently studying at a university.

Your participation is entirely voluntary. You can decide not to take part, stop at any time during the study, or skip a question, without any negative consequences and without giving a reason. If you want your identifiable data to be deleted after participation, you can request this until 19 June 2026. After that date, the link between your identity and the data will be deleted and the dataset will be anonymised, so removal will no longer be possible.

The task is similar to doing a learning activity on a laptop and then answering questions about it. No specific risks are expected. There is no direct personal benefit, but the study may help improve the design of educational games.

For this study, we may collect and process your questionnaire responses, game log data, retention test responses, age, gender, and email address. Your email address will only be used to send the one-week follow-up questionnaire and the written debriefing, and will not be included in the final dataset.

The research data will be stored securely for at least ten years. Pseudonymised questionnaire data and game log data may be archived and later shared with other researchers for non-commercial scientific reuse after the related publication. Before sharing, direct identifiers will be removed and identifying details will be minimised as far as possible. Access will be provided through Dataverse under restricted access, by request and after approval by the current researchers, only to researchers and, where relevant, peer reviewers for scientific purposes. Reuse will be subject to CC BY-NC terms and safeguards prohibiting re-identification, participant contact, and onward sharing. In principle, you have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or objection to the processing of your personal data.
